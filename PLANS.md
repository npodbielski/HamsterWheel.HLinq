# Plans

Open plans in this repo (`plans/`); completed plans move to `plans/done/`. A plan is complete when
everything it designs is merged into `alpha` - a release is not part of a plan, and a plan does not wait
for one.

**Mark a plan done in the MR that completes it**, in its last commit: move the file to `plans/done/`
and update its row here as part of the implementation MR, not as a follow-up chore. The MR is what
lands the design; the row saying so belongs to it. If the MR is later found incomplete, the row moves
back to open in the MR that reopens the work.
