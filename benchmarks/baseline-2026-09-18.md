## Reading

- **Tokenizer share of a full apply is ~1.5–2.5 %.** The tokenizer redesign
  (grammar rules plan) should be justified by extensibility; speed is a bonus,
  not the headline. The Apply numbers are dominated by expression-tree building
  per call (no compiled-query caching) — a separate, much bigger optimization
  opportunity than the tokenizer, but out of scope for this plan.
- **Allocation is heavier than time**: 8–19 KB for sub-10 µs tokenizes (~200–400
  B per query character), and `select27` allocates ~109 KB. Cause: every
  possibility is rescored at every character and each rescan allocates
  (`List`/`OrderBy` per character, `CurrentTokenPossibility` tuples, LINQ
  closures). The S3 span/`MatchAt` design removes per-character rescoring, which
  should mainly show up as allocation reduction.
- `orderbyLower` vs `orderByDescending` confirms the dead-prefix rescoring cost
  (lowercase `orderby` keeps both keywords alive longer) but it is noise-level
  (~1.5 µs) at this scale.
- ShortRun error bars are wide (±10–60 %); compare means with at least that
  tolerance, or re-run with `--job medium` when a decision hangs on the number.
